# Due dates and overdue policy

## Setting due dates

Due dates are calendar dates in YYYY-MM-DD format. A new task cannot be created with a due date in the past. High priority tasks should always have a due date; low priority tasks may have none.

## When a task is overdue

A task is overdue when its due date is earlier than today and its status is not completed. Tasks due today are not overdue yet.

## What to do with overdue tasks

When a task becomes overdue, the owner should either finish it, move the due date with a one-line reason in the description, or lower its priority if it no longer matters. An overdue high priority task must be raised at the next daily stand-up. Tasks that are overdue for more than 14 days are reviewed at weekly triage.
