# How to write a good task

## Good task titles

A good title starts with a verb and names the specific outcome, for example "Fix login redirect loop on Safari" rather than "Login issue". Titles must be between 3 and 120 characters. Avoid vague words like "stuff", "misc" or "various", and do not put the priority or due date in the title because those have their own fields.

## Good descriptions

A description is required. It should explain the problem or goal, why it matters, and what done looks like as acceptance criteria. Add links or steps to reproduce for bugs. Two to five sentences is usually enough.

## One task, one outcome

Split work that has more than one independent outcome into separate tasks so each one can be tracked, prioritised and completed on its own.
