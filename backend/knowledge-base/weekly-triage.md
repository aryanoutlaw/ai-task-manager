# Weekly triage and planning

## When triage happens

The team runs triage every Monday at 10:00. Triage reviews all new pending tasks, all overdue tasks and all low priority tasks untouched for 60 days.

## What triage decides

For each task, triage confirms the priority using the priority levels policy, sets or adjusts the due date, and assigns an owner. Duplicates are merged by completing one task with a note pointing to the other.

## Unassigned high priority work

High priority tasks never wait for Monday triage. They are raised immediately in the team channel and picked up within one business day.
